Bio
======
I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Cybersecurity Group at TU Delft (EEMCS)](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/cybersecurity), where I study the security, privacy, and trustworthiness of Neuro‑Symbolic (NeSy) AI systems and their application to atonomous threat detection and risk-analysis systems. 

I obtained my Ph.D. from the University of Bologna in 2024. My academic background includes research positions at the [SPRITZ Research Group (University of Padua)](https://spritz.math.unipd.it), and visiting researcher roles at Purdue University, hosted by [Dr. Rajiv Khanna](https://rjvak7.github.io) and the [Interactive Intelligence Research Group (TU Delft)](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/intelligent-systems/interactive-intelligence) hosted by [Prof. Catholijn Jonker](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/intelligent-systems/interactive-intelligence/people/current-group-members/catholijn-m-jonker).

My research focuses on bridging machine learning and structured logic to build rigorously stress‑tested AI models that remain reliable under adversarial pressure. In parallel, I apply these trustworthy NeSy agents to cybersecurity, grounding autonomous detection and risk‑analysis systems in structured threat knowledge such as attack graphs and CVE information to improve interpretability and robustness in complex network environments. 

My work has been published in top-tier venues including ICLR, KDD, IEEE Euro S&P, UAI, Elsevier FGCS, Elsevier KBS, IEEE TNSM, ACM CSUR, Springer JAAMAS.

I enjoy collaborating across disciplines (AI security, NeSy, and cyber defense) and I am happy to discuss potential collaborations or student supervision opportunities.
