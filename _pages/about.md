Bio
======
I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the [Multimedia Computing Group](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/intelligent-systems/multimedia-computing) at TU Delft, where I work with [Dr. Megha Khosla](https://khosla.github.io/) on trustworthy and privacy-preserving graph learning. Before that, I spent two years as a postdoc in TU Delft’s [Cybersecurity Group](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/cybersecurity).

My research starts from a simple question: what do AI models really take away from their data, and can we trust what they do with it? Much of my work looks at memorization and privacy, where I have shown how the geometry of the loss surface can estimate which training samples a model memorizes, and how the flatter minima found by sharpness-aware training come with sharper privacy risks. I am now carrying these ideas over to graph learning.

The same question shapes the rest of my work. I stress-test neuro-symbolic AI, which pairs learning with logic, against attacks such as backdoors, and I look for ways to keep learning efficient when it is spread across many devices, as in federated and serverless learning. Cybersecurity is where these threads meet practice: from intrusion detection grounded in attack graphs to whether security experts can trust LLMs to write detection rules.

I obtained my Ph.D. from the University of Bologna in 2024. Along the way, I was a visiting researcher at Purdue University, hosted by [Dr. Rajiv Khanna](https://rjvak7.github.io/), and in the [Interactive Intelligence Group at TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/intelligent-systems/interactive-intelligence), hosted by [Prof. Catholijn Jonker](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/intelligent-systems/interactive-intelligence/people/current-group-members/catholijn-m-jonker). My work has been published at ICLR, KDD, IEEE EuroS&P and UAI, and in journals including ACM CSUR, Elsevier FGCS, Elsevier KBS, IEEE TNSM and Springer JAAMAS.
